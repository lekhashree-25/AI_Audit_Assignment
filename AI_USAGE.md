\# AI Usage Disclosure



\## Purpose



AI assistance was used as a supporting tool for understanding, debugging, repository organization and documentation.



\## Areas of AI Assistance



\### Assignment Understanding

AI assistance was used to understand the assignment structure, requirements and scoring criteria.



\### Git and GitHub

AI assistance was used to understand Git commands, repository organization, branches, commits and GitHub submission.



\### Technical Assistance

AI assistance was used to understand the audit methodology, fertility/tokenization concepts, benchmark interpretation and troubleshooting.



\### Documentation

AI assistance was used to improve the organization and clarity of documentation.



\## Verification



AI-generated suggestions were treated as assistance rather than independent evidence. The final work was reviewed against the project files, corpus, scripts and benchmark data.



\## Transparency



AI assistance did not replace the need to inspect the evidence, understand the calculations and make the final engineering interpretation.
